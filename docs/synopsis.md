# Synopsis - Ganbare! Daiku no Gen-san (SNES, Irem 1993)

Built from a read of the extracted script (`script/`); the game has little story beyond it. Story order:
prologue -> chapters 1-4 (each a first and second half = stage x-1 / x-2) -> finale -> ending.

- **Cast.** Gen-san (Genzo), a hot-headed Edo-style carpenter with a giant wooden mallet. Kanna-chan, his
  girlfriend. The Kuromoku Gang (黒木組): a crooked construction outfit Gen-san beat once before (this is a sequel);
  its head is "the Boss" (社長, an old man who says わし). A rough friend/co-worker of Gen-san's talks with him in the
  panels (calls him "Gen"); an old man (わし, calls him 源よ) asks him for help in chapter 3.
- **Prologue (page0, script0).** The Kuromoku Gang is back; this time they are after Gen-san himself. The Boss
  (who seemed to have reformed) taunts him: now it's Gen-san's turn to "reform".
- **Chapter 1 (Teyande Town).** Gen-san protects the town. A Kansai-dialect Kuromoku thug only knows he was told to
  take Gen-san out.
- **Chapter 2 (Beranme Woods).** The gang threatens the forest; Gen-san searches for the Boss. A woman carpenter of the
  gang attacks. Gen-san learns someone formidable is inside the gang. Kanna-chan worries for him.
- **Chapter 3 (Gatten Valley).** An old rival ("I'm not the man I used to be") challenges Gen-san. Gen-san finally
  tracks down the Boss's hideout.
- **Chapter 3 second half / 4.** At the Kuromoku hideout the Boss has kidnapped Kanna-chan and tells Gen-san to back
  off. The chase goes through time: chapter 4 is "Beyond time" / "Battle in the future"; a future villain calls him
  "Genzo-kun" and boasts of "our power"; the last opponent (Raizo, final stage) mocks his mallet.
- **Ending.** The Boss and his men beg forgiveness and promise to go straight; Gen-san takes Kanna-chan home from
  "this era". Ticker epilogue: the two return; the reformed gang goes back to town to work honestly.

Late reveal to keep: the kidnapping of Kanna-chan is not mentioned before chapter 3/4 (page4, script8).
